Privacy

Alpha version. Last updated October 2026.

What we keep

Your decisions (keep, pass, love, undecided), the works they were about, the reasons you tagged, your settings, and the Curator's Notes written for you. That's it. No name, email, phone number, location, or contacts.

Where it lives

On this device, in your browser's storage. If you turn on sync, a copy is stored on our server under a random collection key so your other devices can join. The key is the only thing that links that copy to you; we never ask who you are.

Curator's Notes

To write a note, a short summary of your taste (for example "likes Impressionism, passes on Baroque") is sent through our server to Anthropic's Claude API. It contains no personal identifiers. Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms.

Museums

Artworks and images load directly from the open-access services of the Art Institute of Chicago, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Victoria and Albert Museum, SMK (National Gallery of Denmark), Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons, and the image servers of the National Gallery of Art and the Cleveland Museum of Art. Those requests go from your device to each source, like visiting their websites. Catalogue records for the National Gallery and Cleveland come from their published open datasets and are served with this app. Picture Plane is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

Victoria and Albert Museum images are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. Museum works shown are public domain or released under CC0. Wikimedia selections are limited to paintings by artists who died before 1955; check each file page on Commons before reusing an image.

Deleting your data

Settings > Start over erases this device. If sync is on, Settings > Delete my synced data removes the server copy immediately and permanently.

No tracking

No ads, no analytics, no third-party trackers.
